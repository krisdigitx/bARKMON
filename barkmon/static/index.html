How recording and processing work
  1. The camera streams audio over RTSP. Video is discarded.
  2. Audio is converted to 16 kHz mono WAV and saved in 60-second files on the persistent volume.
  3. YAMNet analyses completed files. SQLite stores processing state, detections and your reviews; playback uses the original WAV segment.
  4. Older audio and matching records are removed at the retention or storage limit.

Statistics count indexed files; the segment currently being recorded appears after it is saved. Analysis percentage is based on retained audio duration, not wall-clock coverage.
